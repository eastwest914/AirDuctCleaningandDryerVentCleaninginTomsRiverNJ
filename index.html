Frequently Asked Questions

How often should air ducts be cleaned?

Many households benefit from a cleaning every few years, and sooner after a renovation, a pest problem, visible mold concerns or when family members have dust sensitivities. We can tell you what makes sense for your home.


How do I know my dryer vent needs cleaning?

Common signs include clothes that take more than one cycle to dry, a dryer that feels very hot, a burning smell, or little airflow at the outside vent. If you notice any of these, give us a call.
